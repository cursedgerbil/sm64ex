A full list of changes made from sm64ex:

# Build

- BETTERCAMERA and NODRAWINGDISTANCE are set to 1 by default

# Gameplay

- Enabled Better Camera in all Bowser stages
- All stars in the game, excluding stars in the castle and Bowser keys, now prompt a dialog box when picked up that allows the player to either remain in the level or exit via the painting as they normally would when collecting a star. This replaces the save dialog box for 100-coin and secret stars.

# UI

- Added a counter to the top of the screen while a cap powerup is active that shows the number of seconds remaining.
- Added names for all 100-coin stars.
- Renamed the Dire Dire Docks star "Through the Jet Stream" to "Rings of the Jet Stream" to avoid confusion, since a star in Jolly Roger Bay shares the same name.

# Levels

## Bob-omb Battlefield

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving without the wing cap. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 146 to 162, or 101 to 117 without the wing cap.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring in the field.
  - Added an 8-coin ring near the red coin star.
- The Koopa in the field now spawns from Star 2 onwards instead of Star 3 onwards.

## Whomp's Fortress

- Bob-omb Buddy and Hoot The Owl now spawn from Star 2 onwards instead of Star 3 onwards.
- Hoot The Owl now plays the [Owl music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TrqlcurjHPE) from SM64DS while flying.

## Jolly Roger Bay

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 104 to 135.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring around a pillar in the center of the stage.
  - Added an 8-coin ring around the blue coin switch in the cavern. (Slightly moved the switch to accommodate for this.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring below the Metal Cap box in the cavern. (Slightly moved the box to accommodate for this.)
  - The ! box at the beginning of the stage now holds 10 coins instead of 3.

## Cool, Cool Mountain

- Snowman's body now spawns on all stars, not just Snowman's Lost His Head.

## Big Boo's Haunt

- Merry-Go-Round is now active on all stars instead of Star 2 onwards.

## Hazy Maze Cave

- Moved A-Maze-ing Emergency Exit to no longer require using the buggy hangable ceiling.
- Added more coins and enemies to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 139 to 158.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring at the emergency exit platform.
  - Added an 8-coin ring in the red coin room near the moving platform.
  - Added a scuttlebug near the elevator to Dorrie's cavern.
 
## Lethal Lava Land

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star more forgiving without entering the volcano. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 133 to 143, or 107 to 117 without the volcano.)
  - Added a 5-coin line to a floating lava platform.
  - Added a 5-coin line to a platform near the beginning of the stage with a bully on it.
 
## Shifting Sand Land

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star possible without entering the pyramid. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 136 to 172, or 74 to 110 without the pyramid.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring beneath the ! box containing the shell.
  - Added an 8-coin ring at the warp by the oasis.
  - Added a 10-coin ! Box near the tornado by the pyramid entrance.
  - Added a 10-coin ! Box near the cannon in the corner.
- Klepto now spawns holding his star in all stars, not just In The Talons of the Big Bird. (To reduce instances of cap theft.)

## Dire Dire Docks

- The Manta Ray now spawns on all stars instead of Star 2 onwards.
- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving and not require Bowser in the Fire Sea. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 106 to 164, or 60 to 118 without completing Bowser in the Fire Sea.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring on Area 2's seafloor in the back-left corner.
  - Added an 8-coin ring on Area 2's seafloor beneath the entrance.
  - Added an 8-coin ring at the top of Area 1.
  - Added an 8-coin ring above Area 1's whirlpool.
  - Added an 8-coin ring in front of the entrance to Area 2.
  - Added an extra 8-coin ring in the tunnel to Area 2.
  - Added a 10-coin ! Box on the dock by the fire spitter in Area 2.
 
## Snowman's Land

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star not require the igloo. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 127 to 148, or 89 to 110 without the igloo.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring on top of the small hill near the snowman.
  - Added an 8-coin ring on a bump near the Fly Guy.
  - Added a Moneybag near the shell box.

## Wet-Dry World

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 152 to 160, or 107 to 115 without downtown.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring near Chuckya.
- Moved the Heave-Hos back to reduce the chance of Mario getting thrown repeatedly.

## Tall, Tall Mountain

- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star not require the slide. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 137 to 171, or 75 to 109 without the slide.)
  - Added an 8-coin ring before the rolling log.
  - Added an 8-coin ring at Chuckya.
  - Added an 8-coin ring in the leafy pit atop the mountain.
  - Added a 10-coin ! Box atop the mountain.
- Removed the Ukiki by the rolling log. (To reduce instances of cap theft.)
- Made the Monkey Cage spawn in all stars, not just Mystery of the Monkey Cage.
- Made the star atop the mountain spawn in all stars, not just Scale the Mountain.

## Tiny-Huge Island

- The Piranha Plant's turf no longer hinders Mario's jump.
- Removed an over-abundance of Fire Spitters on the tiny island.
  - In particular, removed a Fire Spitter by the cannon and removed a Fire Spitter atop the hill.

## Tick-Tock Clock

- Changed music to [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KgimCwVGiPc) theme.

## Rainbow Ride

- Changed music to [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NAuIdiKDwY0) theme.
- Added a series of rotating platforms to enable skipping all magic carpets across the course with some skill. (God they're slow and dull.)
  - Moved the 1-UP on the way to the big house to fit the new layout.
- Added more coins to make the 100-coin star slightly more forgiving. (Raised the maximum coins collectible in the stage from 146 to 162.)
  - Added two 8-coin rings on the new rotating platforms.

## Tower of the Wing Cap

- Wing Cap given when entering the stage now lasts significantly longer.

## Vanish Cap Under The Moat

- Vanish Cap given when entering the stage now lasts significantly longer.

## Cavern of the Metal Cap

- Metal Cap given when entering the stage now lasts significantly longer.
